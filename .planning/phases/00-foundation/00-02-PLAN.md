<objective>
Define the 8 pure-Pydantic-v2 domain types in `src/shortfire/domain/{market,trading,risk}.py` per D-07..D-15 with construction-time invariants and a Hypothesis property test for every invariant per D-15.

Purpose: These types are the structural backbone of every Phase 1+ feature. They make whole categories of bugs unrepresentable: an Order with intent=close + reduce_only=False cannot be constructed; a RiskLimits with max_per_trade_pct above the RISK-02 5% cap cannot be constructed; a Candle with low > high cannot be constructed; naive datetimes are rejected everywhere.

Output: 3 source files (market.py, trading.py, risk.py) totalling ~250 LOC, with 9 corresponding test files in `tests/unit/domain/` exercising every invariant via Hypothesis.
</objective>
